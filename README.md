## Attribution

Credit for shared work is not guessed from who pushed the commit. Every entry filed
on the dashboard names the member who filed it plus everyone who worked on it, and the
CREDITS screen reads that back by member or by deliverable. That screen is the source
of truth when splitting points for a shared build.
